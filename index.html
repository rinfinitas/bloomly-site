Subscriptions

Bloomly Premium is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple Account. You can manage or cancel it at any time in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad. To restore a purchase on a new device, open Bloomly and tap Restore Purchases.
