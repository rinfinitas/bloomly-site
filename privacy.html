Privacy Policy

Effective 11 October 2026

Bloomly is designed to keep your data on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal information.

Data you enter

Everything you add to Bloomly (beans, recipes, brews, equipment, notes, and photos) is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to any third party. If you use iCloud or computer backups for your device, this data may be included in those backups under Apple's terms.

Camera and photos

If you choose to, Bloomly can use your camera or photo library so you can attach pictures to your entries. Those images stay on your device. Bloomly only accesses photos you take or select.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed entirely by Apple. We don't receive your name, email address, or payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics and tracking

Bloomly contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking, and doesn't use third-party SDKs that collect data.

Children

Bloomly doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.
